📊 About the Metrics

npxG: Non-penalty Expected Goals - likelihood of scoring from shots.
xAG: Expected Assisted Goals - quality of chances created for teammates.

🎯 Key Passes

Passes that directly lead to a shot. A higher rate indicates creative playmaking ability and offensive contribution.

📈 Per 90 Stats

All metrics are normalized per 90 minutes of play, allowing fair comparison between players with different playing time.
